5x leverage
You have ₹1,000 but the broker lends you ₹4,000 more. Now you control a ₹5,000 toy car. A small price move feels 5x bigger to you — because your ₹1,000 is riding on a ₹5,000 thing!

10x leverage — danger zone
You have ₹1,000 and the broker lends you ₹9,000. You're controlling a ₹10,000 position. Now a tiny 10% drop in price completely wipes out all your ₹1,000. This is called liquidation — the broker forcibly closes your trade before you lose their money too.
